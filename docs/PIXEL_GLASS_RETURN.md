# Pixel and glass refinement

October 8, 2026. This visual direction supersedes the rose atmosphere and broad typography changes in the campaign refresh. The founder asked to restore the familiar pixel identity, retain the white-blue closing sections, and refine the existing site.

The original homepage wordmark and white/pink dot wave return on a solid ink background. The idea form, approved film, project walkthrough and white-blue finish remain available below. Studio, Hosting and Registry recover their earlier layouts, headlines and pixel hero fields. A low-opacity pink/white/blue square field animates behind these pages, Pricing and Launch, without adding a homepage background. The existing Manrope typography returns.

The five supplied image references informed dark frosted surfaces, inset edges, etched tiles and bent connections. The new `assets/pixel/plan-console.svg` is an original vector asset, with a subtly etched case, physical connection ports and a pink/white/blue screen. The coin desk connects identity, supply and budget to this console with dark curved cables and traveling pixel signals. Export remains available; wallet approval and pool launch retain dashed connections and explicit planned states. Selecting any of the seven nodes updates its description and real destination. Native keyboard activation remains available.

The glass panels use backdrop blur and restrained reflections with solid neutral surfaces. Selected cards alternate small and larger corners. Pricing retains its white, black and pink plates with readable text. The phone Hosting actions wrap without overlapping.

## Motion and behavior

The existing shared canvas renderer supports the new dust field and ice palette. It keeps its 30 fps cap, device-pixel limit, visibility observers and reduced-motion support. Homepage wave playback follows the shared pause preference, stops offscreen and pauses behind the film. Cable animation sleeps offscreen and in hidden tabs. Reduced motion uses still compositions. The original film and silent white-blue loops remain; rose video is no longer placed in the page heroes.

Project forms, local libraries, uploaded artwork, budgets, exports/imports, watchlists, server controls and account data hooks retain their behavior. No pricing, wallet, billing, session or backend logic was changed. Live transactions remain unavailable in the local launch planner.
